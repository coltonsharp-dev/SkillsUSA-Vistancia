Kit Ecosystems

Students pay into the chosen platform. Prioritize durability, expandability, and documentation.

VEX V5

Precision motion + strong sensors

Best overall for pick-and-place and inspection stations.

REV (FTC)

Modern ecosystem for autonomous work

Best fit for smart warehouse routing and navigation builds.

goBILDA

Heavy-duty metal structure

Best for palletizing, lift mechanisms, and repeatable stacking builds.

TETRIX MAX

Clean education-style modular builds

Strong for multi-station assembly line simulation setups.

Makeblock mBot2

Affordable entry platform

Best as a lower-cost path for inspection/automation basics.
